# Security & responsible use

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please open a private security advisory on GitHub (Security → Report a vulnerability) instead of a public issue.

## Data handling

- ChainGraph has no backend. Cases are stored in your browser (IndexedDB); settings, label libraries and API keys in localStorage.
- Requests go directly from your browser to the public explorers and services listed in the README.
- Images analysed in the OSINT toolkit never leave your machine.

## Responsible use

ChainGraph is built for investigations of financial crime, fraud and scams, for journalism, compliance and research. Attribution and risk scores are heuristic leads that require verification. The tool does not identify the owners of addresses or phone numbers; that requires lawful process with the relevant service. Do not use it to stalk, harass or dox individuals, and follow the laws of your jurisdiction and the terms of the services it queries.
